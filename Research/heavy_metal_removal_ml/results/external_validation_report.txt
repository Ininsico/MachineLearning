INTERPRETATION LIMITS THAT MUST BE CARRIED INTO THE THESIS
  * n = 2 biofilms. They test that the pipeline runs end-to-end on real samples and that the
    outputs are physically plausible; they cannot establish generalisability.
  * The activity score is a curated-literature index aggregated by abundance. It measures
    'how much of this community belongs to genera reported as metal-active', NOT a predicted
    removal percentage for a given metal.
  * Net release of Mn and Ni and the appearance of Cr were observed. A capability-based index
    cannot anticipate release, because release is not represented in the literature label set.
    This is a genuine model limitation, not a measurement artefact.
  * The single strong agreement (Bacillus-dominated SM vs arsenate/arsenite removal) is one
    metal in one sample and is not independent evidence across the nine metals.
